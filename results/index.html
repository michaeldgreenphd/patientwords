Results catalogue

Every run the engine has measured, with its state and what it may be used for.

This page is the full list of what has been measured. It is not a sample and it is not a claim. A run is listed whether its effect is negative, zero or positive, whether it finished or stopped part-way, and whether or not any analysis includes it.

Evidence class, clinical-review status and analysis membership are stated for each run. Rows are next-token measurements of language-model behaviour: the probability of the first encoded target token, which is a lexical proxy. They are not medical advice.

The phrasings are test stimuli written by a model or by hand. They are not statements from patients.
