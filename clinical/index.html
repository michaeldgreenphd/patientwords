Why this belongs in clinical conversations

The register these models penalize is not random. It is the register of lower health literacy — hedged, narrative, metaphor-heavy, sometimes misspelled. That is the same population clinical communication research already treats as at risk, the reason teach-back and literacy-sensitive practice exist. As language models move into triage-adjacent products, phrasing sensitivity becomes a safety property of the deployment, and the people most likely to phrase colloquially are the people with the least slack when advice arrives a tier low.

Two cautions in the other direction. These are next-word measurements on open models, not evaluations of any deployed product; and part of the effect may be the model accommodating the speaker's register rather than losing the concept — the technical page reports the evidence on both sides.

Review status. The care-urgency tier vocabulary behind every number on this page is owner-reviewed, physician review pending. Until a clinician signs off the tier assignments, treat tier-level counts as provisional. The review checklist is a ~30-minute task; the blinded response-coding worksheet (here) takes about 20 more.
